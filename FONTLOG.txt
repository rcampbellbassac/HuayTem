FONTLOG for Huay Tem

Huay Tem (ຫ້ວຍເຕັມ) is a display font for Lao, Thai, Khmer and Latin drawn from a hand-painted
sign photographed in Laos in 2019. Flourished school-wall lettering: tall loops, long swept tails on ຖ ງ ຽ, crisp brush entry ticks.

How it was made
- Lao key letters (ໂ ຮ ງ ຽ ນ ປ ະ ຖ ົ ມ ສ ບ ູ ້ ຫ ວ ຍ ເ ຕ ັ ລ ກ 1 9) were traced from the photograph (scripts/segment.py, scripts/trace.py).
- The other letters were generated on the Noto designs with a simulated brush whose size and the font's width were
  measured from the traced letters (scripts/auto.py), then merged with the traced letters (scripts/hybrid.py).
- Generated letters are drafts to be redrawn toward the traced ones before 1.0.
